Model `UpdateContactRoutingCriteria` (Set routing criteria, tasks/D07), the Type the service takes; the action page's `UpdateRoutingCriteria` is refused as an invalid action type (rule 45 in `conformance/flow-language/actions.md`). The class takes `steps`, a list of one or more steps, with `next` and the catch-all. Each step holds an `expression` and an optional `expiresAfterSeconds`, written as `Expiry.DurationInSeconds` in a decimal string; only the last step may leave it out. Expressions are built by two new helpers: `attributeCondition(name, value, level)` (a predefined attribute's name and value, 1 to 64 characters each, the value also a JSONPath, and a level from 1 to 5, written as a JSON integer with `NumberGreaterOrEqualTo`) and `and(...)` (one to eight requirements). `RoutingExpression` types them, and codegen writes a stored expression back as those calls. The service refuses an `OrExpression` at the top of a step and `Steps` as a JSONPath, so neither is written, and a document holding either stays generic, as does the whole of `RoutingCriteria` as a JSONPath, which the service accepts. The type is legal in inbound, customer queue, transfer to agent and transfer to queue flows and modules, on every channel. In the catalog the expression is a `json` value inside typed steps (docs/adr/0009). The HCL writer and reader take it as the typed `update_contact_routing_criteria` sub-block with `expression = jsonencode(...)`, which no published flowascode provider accepts until the release that carries it. The studio's inspector edits the steps one at a time, each expression in the JSON editor and each expiry in seconds, or shows the whole criteria as a JSONPath. The `unknown-actions` passthrough fixture holds `CompleteOutboundCall` in its Chime voice connector form, which stays generic, carrying its queue token. The 0.3 schema clause holds what the service refused at create: no criteria, no or empty steps, a step without its expression, an OR or a NOT at the top of a step, an expiry without its duration or with a JSONPath one, and a numeric level outside 1 to 5.
