Model the contact-state actions (tasks/D02). `UpdatePreviousContactParticipantState` (Hold customer or agent: `state` is `AgentOnHold`, `CustomerOnHold` or `OffHold`, with `next` and the catch-all) is restricted to inbound and transfer flows and the voice channel, and its 0.3 schema clause holds the three values. `UpdateContactMediaStreamingBehavior` (Start and Stop media streaming: `state` `Enabled` or `Disabled` and the customer's `directions`, `From`, `To` or both; the class writes the one participant and stream type the page supports) is voice only and excluded from hold flows. `UpdateContactMediaProcessing` (Set message processor: `enabled`, a `lambda` reference required when enabled, `deliverUnprocessedMessages`, and both the catch-all and `onChannelMismatch`) is chat only; its Lambda sits at `ChatProcessor.LambdaProcessorARN`, a reference path the schema, `no-literal-arn` and export read like any other, and only the catch-all is required until the service says otherwise. `ResumeContact` is a builder block (`next` and the catch-all, no parameters), a codegen inverter, a catalog entry and an insertable studio block; its catalog `channels` is `TASK`, read from the admin guide's Resume contact block page, so `channel-restricted-action` warns wherever it appears. The HCL writer and reader take them as the typed `update_previous_contact_participant_state`, `update_contact_media_streaming_behavior`, `update_contact_media_processing` and `resume_contact` sub-blocks, which no published flowascode provider accepts until the release that carries it. The `unknown-actions` passthrough fixture holds `LoadContactContent` and `StartVoiceIdStream` in place of the newly modeled types. The service has not yet been asked about these types (rule 41 in `conformance/flow-language/actions.md` is probe pending), so their required branches and flow types are the AWS pages' reading.
